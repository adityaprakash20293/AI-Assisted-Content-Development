AI-assisted transformation

Turn product information into learning content

Generative AI can help organize technical documentation, draft learning objectives, propose a learning sequence, and create an initial assessment.

Example AI prompt
Role: Act as an instructional designer specializing in enterprise SaaS onboarding.

Task: Use the CloudOps product information provided to create a beginner-level onboarding module for new users.

Include:
  • Four measurable learning objectives.
  • A logical sequence of learning topics.
  • Practical software workflow explanations.
  • A quick-reference job aid.
  • Five multiple-choice knowledge-check questions with answer rationales.
Quality requirements: Use only the supplied information. Do not invent product capabilities, permissions, or workflows. Flag missing information for subject matter expert review.

The transformation workflow

1
Source material
Provide approved software documentation.
2
Prompt design
Define the audience, task, format, and constraints.
3
AI-generated draft
Generate objectives, content structure, and questions.
4
Human validation
Review accuracy, usability, and instructional quality.
Key principle: AI supports the drafting process. The instructional designer remains accountable for technical accuracy, learner needs, and final content quality.
